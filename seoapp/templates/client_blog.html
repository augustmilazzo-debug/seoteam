How the blog engine works

  1. Ideas come from your target keywords and the questions people search, ranked by priority. Add your own on the right.
  2. Click Write it. Claude studies the pages ranking for the keyword and writes a full post built for Google and AI answers: quick answer, takeaways, question headings, tables/charts, FAQs, schema and internal links.
  3. Review it, edit anything, ask for a rewrite, then Approve.
  4. Send to WordPress creates a draft in the client's WordPress for final review and publishing. For other platforms, copy or download the HTML.
